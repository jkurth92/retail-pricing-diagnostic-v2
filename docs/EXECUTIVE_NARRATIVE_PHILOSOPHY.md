# Executive Narrative Philosophy

## Storyline, not dashboard

Output reads as a **consulting diagnostic memo section** — connected themes with strategic meaning.

## Good vs bad

**Good:** Explains what structure implies for monetization and coherence.

**Bad:** Benchmark verdicts or tactical price actions.

## Connection rule

Top 2–3 primary themes are woven in the opening narrative; additional themes are listed without over-hedging.

## Context modifiers

- Retailer archetype (`data/executiveNarrativeTemplates.ts` — `ARCHETYPE_NARRATIVE_CONTEXT`)
- Maturity via EPR flows into hypothesis confidence, then storyline confidence summary

## No recommendations

Narratives stop at thematic opportunity pools. They do not say “raise prices” or “cut promos by X%.”
