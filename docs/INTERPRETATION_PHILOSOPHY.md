# Interpretation Philosophy

## Consulting-style, explainable engine

The diagnostic is designed as a **structured consulting workflow**, not operational pricing software:

- White cards, navy text, restrained blue accents
- Explicit guardrails on every analysis tab
- Deterministic inference with visible rationale and confidence
- No black-box AI pricing logic

## Competitor matching not required for POC

Peer suggestions support **retailer overview context** only. Diagnostic inference uses:

- Client-selected archetype and posture
- Strategic objectives
- Normalized upload field coverage (preview)

Competitive benchmarks and matcher engines are **out of scope** until explicitly approved.

## Progression of intelligence

```text
Uploads → Normalization → Pattern features (descriptive)
       → Knowledge registry (structural expectations)
       → Rules (interpretive, future)
       → Opportunity (financial, future)
```

## What we do not infer in Step 5B

- Whether pricing is good or bad
- Optimal promo or markdown depth
- Elasticity or revenue uplift
- Automated price changes
- SKU-level operational recommendations

## Overrides

Category and item roles are **strategic placeholders**. Lightweight override is expected when client taxonomy differs; the app does not assume SKU-by-SKU management.
