# Opportunity Philosophy

## Thematic, not optimized

Opportunity in Step 6A represents **recoverable strategic value pools** attached to hypotheses — not SKU-level or price-point recommendations.

## Margin-first

| Theme | Illustrative margin band |
|-------|-------------------------|
| Architecture | 0.5%–1.5% |
| KVI efficiency | 0.2%–0.7% |
| Promo efficiency | 0.3%–1.0% |
| Markdown lifecycle | 0.2%–0.8% |

Bands are in `data/opportunityCalibrationBands.ts` and adjusted by confidence — not by client revenue in this build.

## Revenue sensitivity

Revenue sensitivity is **directional and secondary** — noted in opportunity themes but not modeled as volume optimization.

## Elasticity

Elasticity sensitivity (`low` | `moderate` | `high`) modifies **interpretation framing** only. It does not drive elasticity math or price changes.

## No fake precision

Dollar totals, bps of revenue in scope, and optimized ranges are intentionally **not calculated** until explicit alignment.
