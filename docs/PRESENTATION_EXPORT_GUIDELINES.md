# Presentation Export Guidelines

## Design intent

Deck exports should feel **McKinsey-style and executive-ready**: storyline-first, takeaway-first, sparse, with intentional whitespace and RHS strategic implication callouts.

## Slide order (default)

1. **Executive Summary & Opportunity** — answer-first headline, lever breakdown, total margin range, RHS takeaway
2. **Architecture Deep Dive** — anchor slide; observations, evidence, premiumization/ladder, RHS implication
3. **Supporting Structural Themes** — KVI, promotions, governance (each with RHS callout, not separate implication slides)
4. **Optional appendix** — retailer-specific (markdown lifecycle, zoning, apparel) when profile signals warrant it

## Do not

- Add dashboard-style or chart-heavy slides
- Export static images instead of editable shapes
- Hardcode proprietary assets beyond `templates/Template.pptx`
- Include operational recommendations or exact price points

## Template integration

Use `templates/Template.pptx` for layout philosophy (typography hierarchy, navy palette, callout box). Named shapes: `Title`, `Headline`, `Body`, `Callout`.

## Consultant workflow

Download PPTX → refine narrative in PowerPoint → integrate into LOP or client workshop materials. The app preview mirrors slide structure before export.
