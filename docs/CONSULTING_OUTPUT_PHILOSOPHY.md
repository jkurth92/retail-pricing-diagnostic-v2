# Consulting Output Philosophy

## First drafts, not finals

All exports are **high-quality consulting first drafts** — modular, editable, and consultant-refinable. They are not analytics reports, static dashboards, or optimization outputs.

## Narrative-first

Outputs prioritize:

1. **Answer-first** structure (memo executive answer; slide 1 opportunity headline)
2. **Architecture-first** storyline (architecture deep dive as anchor slide)
3. **Opportunity-led** framing (directional margin bands, not fake precision)
4. **Executive-readable** tone (strategic, concise, fact-based)

## Formats

- **DOCX** is preferred over PDF in this build — easier consultant editing in Word.
- **PPTX** remains fully editable in PowerPoint and LOP-ready.
- **Email** is partner-level and relationship-oriented — not automated outreach.

## Diagnostic, not prescriptive

Outputs explain structural opportunity and leadership questions. They do **not** include operational recommendations, competitor matching, or exact pricing actions.

## Modularity

`ExportSection` and `ExportSectionSelection` support future section editing, selective slide generation, and narrative refinement without locking layouts.
